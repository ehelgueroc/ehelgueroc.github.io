## Question

What does the page say about itself to browsers, search engines and link previews? Decide:

- `<title>` and meta description, plus the canonical URL (the site lives at https://ehelgueroc.github.io per **Choose the hosting platform**)
- the Open Graph / Twitter card: the text, and whether the image is a static designed card (name + title on the Variant C gradient) or just the photo
- the favicon (initials on the gradient? the photo?) and `theme-color` for light and dark
- whether to add basic Person structured data (JSON-LD) for search

## Resolution

- **`<title>`**: "Esteban Helguero Cardiff · Senior Backend Engineer", built from `intro.md` as name + title.
- **Meta description** (149 characters, stored as `description` in `src/content/intro.md`): "Senior Backend Engineer with 15+ years of experience: Node.js and TypeScript on AWS, platform tooling, Kafka and Terraform. Remote from Gran Canaria."
- **Canonical URL**: built from Astro's `site`, currently `https://ehelgueroc.github.io/`, so a custom domain later is a one-line change.
- **Link previews**: Open Graph (`og:type=profile`, title, description, URL, image) plus `twitter:card = summary_large_image`.
- **Preview image**: a designed 1200×630 card on the dark background: name, "Senior Backend Engineer", the tech subline, a violet→cyan accent, and the URL. It's generated once with `sharp` from an SVG by `scripts/generate-images.mjs`, committed as `public/og.png`, and regenerated by hand when the content or photo changes.
- **Favicon**: "EH" in white on a violet→cyan rounded square. `public/favicon.svg` is for modern browsers, plus `apple-touch-icon.png` (180×180) and `favicon-32.png` from the same script.
- **`theme-color`**: `#f6f7fb` under `prefers-color-scheme: light`, and `#0e0f16` under dark.
- **JSON-LD `Person`**: name, jobTitle, url, homeLocation (from `intro.md`), `sameAs` (the non-email links in `intro.md`), and `knowsAbout` (the current role's Tech Tags).
- **No sitemap and no robots.txt**: a single page with nothing to hide adds nothing.

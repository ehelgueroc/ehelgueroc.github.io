## Question

What exactly goes in the Timeline? `Profile.pdf` lists 13 roles (2005 to now). Some are in Spanish, some overlap (Samtec inside SPI Fuerteventura, Indra/INERZA), and one has no description (Destinum Networks). Decide:

- which roles get their own Timeline Entry, and whether early roles (2005–2014) are merged into one "Early career" entry
- the English wording of each entry's collapsed line and its Expanded Entry details (how many bullets, what tone)
- the tech tags per entry
- whether education appears in the Timeline

## Resolution

The Timeline has six Timeline Entries, newest first. Full English wording is in [`content/timeline.md`](../content/timeline.md), which the owner approved.

- **Own entries**: rindus (2023–present), Secret Source (2022–2023), redk (2017–2022), Coolcan Ideas (2016–2017), Open Canarias (2015–2016).
- **One Period Entry**: "Early career", 2005–2015, merging Destinum, INERZA, Indra, SPI Fuerteventura, Samtec, Bull and Confederación de Empresarios. It's grouped on purpose (see `GLOSSARY.md`).
- **Collapsed view**: years · title · company · one-line summary · up to 5 Tech Tags (visible without expanding).
- **Expanded view**: location + 3–5 outcome-focused bullets.
- **Clients** are named exactly as on LinkedIn: C&A is named, and Secret Source's client stays "a UK client".
- **redk**: keeps serverless, Node.js, the SugarCRM and Zendesk certifications, and Scrum Master. IBM Watson is dropped.
- **Education** is not in the Timeline. Whether it appears in the Intro is up to **Define the Intro content**.
- Bullets only reword `Profile.pdf` and add no new claims or metrics.

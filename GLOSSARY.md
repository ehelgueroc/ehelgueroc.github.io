# Glossary

**Intro**: the opening section of the page, with name, headline, a short summary, a photo, and contact/social links.

**Timeline**: the owner's career in reverse-chronological order (newest first), shown as Timeline Entries in a vertical line.

**Timeline Entry**: one role at one employer (title, company, dates, location), _or_ a Period Entry. Shown collapsed by default. _Not_ a project or a skill.

**Period Entry**: a Timeline Entry that groups several related earlier roles at different employers into one period (e.g. "Early career, 2005–2015"). It's grouped on purpose and shouldn't be split back into individual roles.

**Expanded Entry**: a Timeline Entry the visitor has clicked open to see its details.

**Tech Tag**: a short technology label on a Timeline Entry (e.g. "Kafka"), visible while the entry is collapsed. There are at most five per entry.
